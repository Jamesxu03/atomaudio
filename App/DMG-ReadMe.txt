AUDIO INPUT — free, private dictation for your Mac

Hold a key, speak English, let go: your words are typed wherever your cursor is.
Everything runs on your Mac. No audio or text is ever sent anywhere.

You need a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 26 or later.


INSTALL (about 2 minutes, once)

1. Drag "Audio Input" onto the "Applications" folder in this window.

2. Open your Applications folder and double-click Audio Input.
   macOS will say "Apple could not verify Audio Input is free of malware".
   That's because the app is shared between friends rather than sold through
   Apple, so it hasn't been checked by Apple. Click "Done".

3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security. Scroll down to "Security",
   where it says Audio Input was blocked, and click "Open Anyway".
   Enter your Mac password, then click "Open Anyway" once more.

4. When asked, allow Microphone access.

5. Turn on Audio Input in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.
   It needs this to notice the key you hold, read the text before your cursor
   (so it can tell "app" from "act"), and paste your words.

A ring of coral dots appears in the menu bar at the top of the screen. The very
first time, it stays faint for up to a minute while your Mac prepares the speech
model. After that it's ready within a few seconds of launching.


USE

Click into any text box. Hold the Right Option (⌥) key, speak, and let go.

Click the dots in the menu bar to:
- choose a different key to hold (Dictation Key)
- start Audio Input when you log in (Launch at Login)
- turn the AI clean-up on or off
- add your own names and jargon (Edit Word List…)

The AI clean-up fixes punctuation and misheard words. It uses Apple
Intelligence, which also runs on your Mac. Turn it on in System Settings →
Apple Intelligence & Siri. Without it, dictation still works.


UPDATING TO A NEW VERSION

Quit Audio Input from its menu, then drag the new version into Applications
and replace the old one. Open it the same way as in step 2–3 above. In
Privacy & Security → Accessibility, select Audio Input, remove it with the
"–" button, open the app again and turn it back on.


UNINSTALL

Quit Audio Input from its menu and move it to the Trash. To also remove your
word list, delete the folder ~/Library/Application Support/Audio Input.
